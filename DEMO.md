# 🎬 ShopPilot AI — 5-Minute Hackathon Presentation & Demo Script

**Track**: Track 1: AI Growth & Agentic Commerce  
**Event**: Razorpay AI Builder Internship 2026  
**Target Duration**: 5 Minutes  

---

## ⏱️ Step-by-Step Presentation Script

### Minute 0:00 - 0:45 | Hook & Value Proposition
> **Presenter**: "Judges and team, traditional e-commerce has a conversion problem. Customers type natural queries like *'I need a laptop for coding under 60k'*, but traditional stores force them through rigid filters and endless generic listings. Meanwhile, standard AI chatbots just talk — they don't drive revenue or complete transactions safely."

*Action*: Open **ShopPilot AI** homepage (`http://localhost:3000`). Highlight the bright, modern SaaS design, hero headline *"Turn what customers want into what they buy"*, and the *"✦ AI-Powered Commerce"* badge.

---

### Minute 0:45 - 1:30 | Intent Understanding & Agentic Recommendation
> **Presenter**: "Let's launch AI Shopping with a real customer scenario."

*Action*: 
1. Click **"Try AI Shopping →"** or select the prompt:
   `"I need a laptop for coding under ₹60,000"`
2. Point out the **ShopPilot Activity Engine** component on screen showing real-time agent execution:
   - 🤖 *Understanding customer intent* (Budget: ₹60,000 | Category: Laptops)
   - 🔎 *Searching product catalog*
   - 🧠 *Ranking best matches* (Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 Intel i5 at 96% score)
3. Show the **"Best match for you"** product card.
4. Click **"Why this product?"** button.
   - Explain: *"Notice how ShopPilot provides explicit reasoning breakdown: within budget ₹60,000, 16GB RAM for coding, 512GB SSD, and 4.6 star customer rating."*

---

### Minute 1:30 - 2:15 | Smart Upsell & Merchant Revenue Growth
> **Presenter**: "Here is where ShopPilot transforms agentic discovery into merchant growth."

*Action*:
1. Scroll down to **"Complete your setup?"** section.
2. Point out the recommended add-ons:
   - *Logitech Wireless Mouse* (₹799)
   - *Ergonomic Aluminum Laptop Stand* (₹1,299)
3. Click **"+ Add to Setup"** on both items.
4. Point out the **Real-time Side-by-Side Cart Arithmetic Box**:
   - Original Laptop: **₹54,999**
   - Added Upsell Value: **+₹2,098**
   - New Cart Total: **₹57,097**
5. Highlight banner: *"ShopPilot identified ₹2,098 in relevant add-ons without being intrusive or coercive."*

---

### Minute 2:15 - 3:15 | Bounded Action Safety & Razorpay Payment Consent
> **Presenter**: "AI agents must never perform unauthorized financial transactions. ShopPilot enforces bounded payment safety."

*Action*:
1. Click **"Continue to Secure Checkout"**.
2. Show the **Explicit Consent Gate Modal**:
   - Modal title: *"Ready to complete your order?"*
   - Point out the transaction guardrail banner: *"Bounded Action Rule — Max limit ₹1,00,000"*.
   - Check the explicit agreement checkbox.
3. Click **"Confirm & Pay"**.
4. Show the **Razorpay Test Mode / Demo Payment Modal**:
   - Click **"Simulate Successful Payment"**.
5. Watch the celebratory confetti burst and inspect the **Payment Success Screen**:
   - Show *Order ID*, *Payment ID*, *Amount (₹57,097)*, and *How ShopPilot Helped* audit checklist.

---

### Minute 3:15 - 4:15 | Visual Audit Trail & Merchant Growth Analytics
> **Presenter**: "Every autonomous agent action must be completely audit-verifiable."

*Action*:
1. Click **"Audit Trail"** in top navbar.
2. Walk through the vertical timeline showing sequential logs:
   - `USER_INTENT` → `PRODUCT_SEARCH` → `RECOMMENDATION` → `UPSELL_SUGGESTED` → `USER_CONFIRMATION` → `PAYMENT_ORDER_CREATED` → `PAYMENT_SUCCESS`.
3. Click **"Merchant Insights"** in top navbar.
4. Show the **Merchant Growth Dashboard**:
   - Top metrics: Total Revenue, AI-Influenced Revenue, Upsell Revenue, Orders, Conversion Rate (47.7%), Average Order Value (AOV).
   - Recharts visual graphs: Weekly Revenue Growth & AI Impact.
   - Conversion Funnel: *Customer Intent → Recommendation → Product View → Upsell → Checkout → Payment*.
   - AI Growth Insights cards highlighting sales lift and cross-sell contributions.

---

### Minute 4:15 - 5:00 | Graceful Failure Handling & Closing
> **Presenter**: "What happens if a payment fails? ShopPilot never crashes or hangs in a loop."

*Action*:
1. Open **AI Shopping**, add an item, click checkout.
2. In Demo Payment Modal, click **"Simulate Failed Payment"**.
3. Show the polished failure view: *"Payment wasn't completed. No successful payment has been recorded."*
4. Show the **"Try Again"** and **"Return to Cart"** buttons, and point out the `PAYMENT_FAILED` audit log entry.
5. **Closing Summary**:
   > *"ShopPilot AI proves that agentic commerce can understand human intent, grow merchant revenue through intelligent cross-selling, and safely execute verified transactions via Razorpay. Thank you!"*
